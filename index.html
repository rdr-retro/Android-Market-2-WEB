Historical Preservation of Android applications

Any Android device deserves to keep working and not end up in the trash due to a lack of compatible software. This page was created to collect and preserve classic apps and games, making it easier for old phones and tablets to remain useful. Our goal is the historical and technical preservation of the Android ecosystem: documenting, organizing, and keeping accessible applications that are hard to find today. This initiative does not seek to promote piracy, but rather responsible preservation and offline use on old Android devices.
